Who we are

About ACTPAbout ACTP

A diversified hospitality portfolio and strategic partnerships, from the Nile to the Red Sea.

ACTP operates across the tourism and real estate sectors. It owns hotels outright, invests alongside the world's leading hotel brands, and runs the commercial assets that keep its destinations alive all year.

Every investment follows one idea: turn a destination into a landmark that lasts, and grow it with discipline.

Parent companyEst. 1981

LAFICO brings strategic investment expertise and a long view to everything ACTP builds.

The Libyan Foreign Investment Company, established in 1981, is ACTP's parent company, supporting its long-term vision for hospitality and tourism in Egypt.
