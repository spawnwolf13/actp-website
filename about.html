About

A subsidiary of LAFICO, investing in Egypt's tourism and real estate since 1990.

Who we are

Transforming tourism destinations into enduring landmarks.

Since 1990, the Arab Company for Touristic Projects (ACTP), a subsidiary of LAFICO, has been one of the leading companies in tourism investment, operating across the tourism and real estate sectors through a diversified hospitality portfolio and strategic partnerships in Egypt.

ACTP focuses on transforming destinations into enduring landmarks, combining investment expertise with a long-term vision for sustainable growth.

Our vision

To pioneer enduring tourism destinations that become distinctive landmarks through sustainable practices.

Our mission

To drive long-term value in Egypt's tourism sector through strategic hospitality investment, destination development, and operational excellence.
